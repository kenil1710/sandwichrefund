# Attack report

An attack round on all three layers before deployment: the contracts (`test/test_attacks.py`, plus the rule tests
in `test/test_rule.py`), the keeper (`test/keeper.test.mjs`) and meaning (`test/test_meaning.py`: docs, the
mirror of the rule, the gate). Every High and Medium finding was fixed before the contracts were deployed; each
row names the test that holds it.

| id | attack | severity | status | held by |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | **Self-sandwich to farm refunds, linked addresses.** The attacker is the victim (same `tx.from`), the victim's swap pays the attacker's contract, the attacker's swaps pay the victim, or the victim transaction is sent by the bot contract. | High | Fixed: rule 3.7 rules UNCLEAR SELF_SANDWICH when the victim's `tx.from` or its swap recipient is any EOA, contract (`to`) or recipient of the pair; a pair formed by the victim's own EOA cannot form at all (the victim's transaction is excluded from the pairs, and its EOA links the bot's swaps to it). | `FakeVictims.*`, `SelfSandwich.*` |
| A1b | **Self-sandwich with unlinked fresh wallets.** A farmer sandwiches a second wallet it controls, after a pool's terms are public. | Low | Design choice: the rule refuses every link visible on chain (A1); exposure to a wallet with no on-chain link is set by the sponsor's terms. The farmer must really move the price on a factory-listed mainnet pool (three transactions of gas plus LP fees on two swaps), each refund is capped by `max_per_swap`, each address by `max_per_victim`, and the pool balance caps the total. A pool whose block range ended before it was created has no such exposure: the canonical pool (blocks 26230913-26232413) was created after its range. /docs and the sponsor page state this for forward-looking pools. | `test_a1_unlinked_wallet_is_bounded_by_caps` |
| A2 | **Look-alike pool.** A contract emits Uniswap Swap events with a victim's numbers, or claims a real factory in `factory()`. | High | Fixed: a pool counts only if its `factory()` is a listed factory AND that factory's `getPair` / `getPool(token0, token1, fee)` returns the pool itself; anything else is POOL_NOT_CANONICAL. Swap events of the wrong kind for a pool, or with the wrong topic count, are ignored. | `LookAlikes.*`, `Coverage.test_lookalike_pool` |
| A3 | **Inflated profit via fee-on-transfer tokens** or huge amounts. | Medium | Fixed: the loss counts only in a token the sponsor priced (PROFIT_TOKEN_NOT_PRICED otherwise); amounts are the pool's own Swap amounts (what the pool received and paid), so a fee on the other side cannot inflate the priced side; refunds are capped per swap. A sponsor pricing a fee-on-transfer token is told not to on /docs. | `Inflation.*` |
| A4 | **Junk cases.** Random hashes filed to fill storage or the board. | Low | They cost the filer a transaction each, are rate-limited per (pool, sender), are recorded UNVERIFIABLE and move no GEN. | `Griefing.test_a4_junk_*` |
| A4b | **Locking a victim out.** A griefer opens a tiny pool covering the same routers and files real victims first, so they become SANDWICHED_UNPAID, final. | High | Fixed during this round: a case is (pool, chain, hash); each pool decides each case once, and `get_case` reports a transaction's strongest decision. The real sponsor's pool still decides and pays. | `test_griefer_tiny_pool_cannot_lock_a_victim_out`, `test_a4_tiny_pool_cannot_lock_victims` |
| A4c | **Locking a case as unreadable.** Filing a real case before its block is final, or during an RPC outage. | Medium | Fixed: UNVERIFIABLE (and NOT_COVERED) are not final; the case is filed again. | `test_a4_filing_a_real_case_unfinalized_does_not_lock_it`, `test_a4_rpc_outage_does_not_lock_a_case` |
| A5 | **Keeper duplication.** Two keepers, a crash after submitting, a resubmitted hash, or spelling variants of a hash. | Medium | Fixed: the contract refuses a decided case (ALREADY_DECIDED_<state>), hashes and chains are normalized, the keeper reads the case before filing, checkpoints every hash at once and never submits while its last transaction is not terminal. | `Duplicates.*`, keeper `two keepers...`, `crash after submit...` |
| A6 | **Forged consensus answers.** A leader inflates amounts, swaps the victim, claims unreadable, or sends garbage. | High | Fixed: every validator reads both RPCs itself and must get identical facts; an exception in the validator is a disagreement (found in this round: a non-dict `facts` raised; now caught). A leader claiming "unfinalized" can only produce UNVERIFIABLE, which is resubmittable. | `Forgery.*`, `Conservation.test_undetermined_round_commits_nothing` |
| A7 | **Value leaks.** A second way out, an internal transfer that Studio skips, a refund to the filer instead of the victim. | High | Fixed: one `emit_call` in `_send`, four callers (refund, close, credit, stray); payouts go to `tx.from`; the ledger is checked after every step. | `Leaks.*`, `Conservation.*`, `test_static.ValueExits` |
| A8 | **Keeper fed by one lying endpoint.** | Medium | Fixed: a page of logs or a transaction counts only when two endpoints answered identically; otherwise the page is retried and shown on /keeper. | keeper `a lying endpoint...`, `endpoints disagreeing...` |
| A9 | **RPC rate limits.** Every validator reads at once; the feasibility probe hit HTTP 429 reading three cases per transaction. | Medium | Fixed: one case per transaction; a refusal is UNVERIFIABLE and retried by the keeper with backoff. | `Rpc.*`, keeper `UNVERIFIABLE decision is retried...` |
| A10 | **One pair, several victims.** A bundle front, v1, v2, back would refund the same profit twice. | Medium | Fixed: MULTIPLE_VICTIMS rules both UNCLEAR. | `Ambiguous.test_two_victims_in_one_bundle` |
| A11 | **Docs that say something the code does not.** | Medium | Fixed: every reason in the code must appear in docs/RULES.md, every constant with its value, the modes table, the refund and profit formulas; the site's /docs page is generated from docs/RULES.md and checked by hash; the rule's JavaScript mirror gives the same verdict as the contract on all 65 real cases and every fixture. | `test_meaning.DocsTruth`, `test_meaning.Mirror`, `test_meaning.RealCases` |
| A12 | **Gate consumers misled by missing data.** | High | Fixed: unfiled, unreadable, inconsistent or incomplete answers are UNVERIFIABLE (or NO_POOL), never `clean` or `refunded`; an address with no cases is never CLEAN; 600 random corruptions of a registry answer never produce a favorable reading. | `test_gate.*`, `test_meaning.NeverFavorable` |
| A13 | **A sponsor withdrawing after victims relied on the pool.** | High | Fixed by design: no cancel, no withdraw, no term change; only top-up and later deadlines; the remainder leaves only by `close_pool` after the deadline. | `Lifecycle.test_no_cancel_method`, `test_static.ValueExits.test_no_owner_powers` |
| A14 | **A filer that does not fund the payout.** | Low | Design choice: the refund's external message needs an allocation naming the victim; without it the call fails and records nothing. The keeper always attaches it. | on chain: docs/SEEDS.md |

All High and Medium findings are fixed; the two Low rows are design choices with the bounds stated.
